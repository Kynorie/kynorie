hwreport

A simple CLI tool that displays your computer's hardware and system information.

[linux@user ~]$ hwreport

CPU | AMD Ryzen 7 PRO 3700U w/ Radeon Vega Mobile Gfx
GPU | Advanced Micro Devices, Inc. [AMD/ATI] Picasso/Raven 2 [Radeon Vega Series / Radeon Vega Mobile Series] (rev d1)
RAM | 13.5 GB
DISPLAY | N/A
BATTERY | 100%
OS | Arch Linux
KERNEL | 7.2.4-arch1-2

If something here isn't correct, or isn't displaying. Let me know!
